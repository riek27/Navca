The Impact of Your Gift

Where Your Donation Goes

We are committed to transparency. Every dollar is directed toward programs that create real, measurable change.

Education

School supplies, teacher stipends, and safe learning spaces for children in conflict-affected areas.

$25 / educates 1 child for a month

Protection

GBV response, psychosocial support, child protection services, and safe houses for survivors.

$50 / supports 1 survivor with counseling

Livelihoods

Seed distribution, vocational training, and small business grants for women and youth.

$100 / jumpstarts a small business

Emergency Relief

Food, clean water, shelter materials, and essential supplies during crises and displacement.

$75 / supports a displaced family
