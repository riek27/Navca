Collaboration & Coordination

Partnerships That
Strengthen Our Impact

NAVCA works collaboratively with humanitarian actors, government institutions, donors, networks, and communities to deliver sustainable and inclusive development programs across South Sudan.

Building stronger communities through collaboration and shared responsibility.

Why Partnerships Matter

No organization can address South Sudan's challenges alone.

NAVCA believes that meaningful change is achieved through strategic partnerships that combine expertise, resources, and local knowledge to reach vulnerable populations more effectively.

Through partnerships and coordination mechanisms, we:

  • Improve humanitarian response
  • Strengthen community resilience
  • Share knowledge and best practices
  • Promote accountability and transparency
  • Avoid duplication of services
  • Reach more vulnerable people

Humanitarian Cluster Memberships

NAVCA actively participates in national and sub-national humanitarian coordination mechanisms.

Food Security & Livelihoods (FSL) Cluster

Supporting food security interventions, agricultural livelihoods, economic empowerment, and resilience-building activities.

WASH Cluster

Promoting access to safe water, sanitation, hygiene services, and disease prevention programs.

Gender-Based Violence (GBV) Cluster

Working to prevent and respond to violence against women and girls while strengthening protection systems.

Protection Cluster

Supporting vulnerable populations through protection monitoring, advocacy, referrals, and safeguarding interventions.

Child Protection Cluster

Promoting child rights, child safeguarding, family tracing, psychosocial support, and child-friendly services.

Education Cluster

Supporting access to quality education and learning opportunities for vulnerable children and youth.

NFI / LHP / CCM Cluster

Supporting emergency response activities through coordination of non-food items, shelter interventions, and camp coordination efforts.

Networks & Alliances

NAVCA is a proud member of several national and international networks that promote accountability, safeguarding, and locally-led development.

C4C

Charter for Change (C4C)

Supporting locally-led humanitarian action and strengthening the role of national organizations in humanitarian response.

PSEA Network

Committed to safeguarding, accountability, and preventing all forms of exploitation and abuse.

Child Alliance Network

Collaborating with child-focused organizations to advance child protection, education, and wellbeing initiatives.

Partnership Approach

NAVCA's partnership model is guided by:

1

Mutual Respect

Recognizing the strengths and expertise of all partners.

2

Accountability

Maintaining transparency in program implementation and resource management.

3

Inclusion

Ensuring participation of women, youth, persons with disabilities, and marginalized groups.

4

Sustainability

Building long-term solutions that remain effective beyond project timelines.
