Flagship Achievement

USAID Youth Empowerment Activity (YEA)

One of NAVCA's notable achievements was supporting the implementation of the USAID Youth Empowerment Activity, a comprehensive multi-sector project.

Integrated Project Components

  • GBV Prevention & Response

    Strengthened protection mechanisms and survivor-centered support.

  • Education Support

    Expanded access to quality learning opportunities for vulnerable youth.

  • Sexual & Reproductive Health

    Improved health outcomes through awareness and services.

  • Peacebuilding Activities

    Fostered social cohesion and conflict resolution among youth.

USAID

This initiative contributed to creating safer, healthier, and more resilient communities while empowering youth to become positive agents of change.

Program Achievements

What We Have Accomplished

Protection & GBV

  • Conducted community awareness sessions on GBV prevention and child protection.
  • Supported protection initiatives for vulnerable groups including women and children.
  • Promoted safer environments through advocacy and referral pathways.

Youth Empowerment

  • Delivered entrepreneurship and livelihood training for vulnerable youth.
  • Supported youth participation in community development initiatives.
  • Promoted economic self-reliance through "Be Your Own Boss" programs.

Peacebuilding

  • Facilitated community dialogue initiatives across conflict-affected areas.
  • Strengthened social cohesion among diverse communities.
  • Encouraged peaceful conflict resolution through local peace committees.

Education

  • Supported access to learning opportunities for vulnerable children and youth.
  • Promoted inclusion of children with disabilities in education programs.
  • Encouraged youth skills development through vocational training.
