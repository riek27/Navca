About NAVCA

A Registered National NGO Committed to the Most Vulnerable

The Nation Wide Vulnerability Concern Agency (NAVCA) is a women-led National Non-Governmental Organization registered with the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC) of the Government of South Sudan under the NGOs Act, 2016 — Registration Number 4480, established on 22nd September 2022. The organization is currently headed by a female Chief Executive Officer.

Our Vision

To foster a safe, peaceful, and inclusive society that upholds human dignity, protects the environment, promotes access to basic socio-economic services, and advances gender equality and human rights.

Our Mission

To improve the quality of life of the most vulnerable communities in South Sudan, especially women, children, the elderly, and persons with disabilities, through integrated, inclusive, and rights-based humanitarian and development interventions.

The Context

Responding to a Protracted Crisis

South Sudan faces a protracted humanitarian crisis driven by decades of conflict, political instability, and frequent climate-related disasters — flooding and drought. This has resulted in mass displacement, with millions displaced or having fled to neighboring countries, many returning to areas with minimal basic services.

Vulnerable groups — women, children, youth, the elderly, and persons with disabilities — face disproportionate challenges due to systemic discrimination, gender inequality, and negative societal attitudes. NAVCA exists to bridge this gap.

Flagship Project

USAID Youth Empowerment Activity (YEA)

NAVCA successfully implemented a comprehensive project integrating GBV/Protection, Education, Sexual Reproductive Health, and Peace-Building — funded by USAID's Youth Empowerment Activity.

The project leveraged USAID investment to foster a generation of healthy, educated, and protected youth who are actively participating in peace-building to transform conflict-affected areas into stable, thriving communities.
