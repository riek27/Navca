About NAVCA

Transforming Lives Through Compassion, Inclusion & Sustainable Development

The Nation Wide Vulnerability Concern Agency (NAVCA) is a registered South Sudanese non-governmental organization committed to improving the lives of vulnerable communities through humanitarian and development programs.

Rooted in the communities we serve, we work alongside women, children, youth, persons with disabilities, and displaced populations to restore dignity, unlock opportunity, and build resilience. Our rights-based approach ensures that every program we deliver is participatory, inclusive, and sustainable.

Our Programs & Areas of Work

Twelve integrated program areas working together to build resilient, empowered, and self-reliant communities across South Sudan.

Our Journey of Impact

Key milestones in our mission to transform lives across South Sudan.

Milestone 01

Community Outreach

Reaching remote and underserved communities with life-saving humanitarian assistance and development programs.

Milestone 02

Youth Empowerment

Training young South Sudanese in vocational skills, entrepreneurship, and leadership to drive national development.

Milestone 03

Education Programs

Establishing learning spaces, training teachers, and providing scholarships to unlock potential in every child.

Milestone 04

Peacebuilding Activities

Facilitating dialogue, reconciliation, and social cohesion initiatives to heal divisions and build lasting peace.

Milestone 05

Women Empowerment Initiatives
