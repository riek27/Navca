Where We Make A Difference

Areas of Impact

Protection & GBV Prevention

Strengthening protection systems and promoting safer environments for women, children, and vulnerable populations through awareness, advocacy, and survivor-centered support.

Education & Skills Development

Supporting learning opportunities, vocational training, and youth empowerment initiatives that open doors to brighter futures for vulnerable children and young people.

Livelihoods & Economic Empowerment

Helping vulnerable households improve income opportunities through VSLA groups, small business development, agricultural support, and livelihood diversification.

Peacebuilding & Social Cohesion

Facilitating dialogue, reconciliation, and peaceful coexistence among communities through peace committees, youth initiatives, and community engagement.

Health, Nutrition & WASH

Improving access to health information, sanitation, hygiene, nutrition awareness, and essential services to promote community wellbeing and prevent disease.

Data Collection & Research

Conducting baseline surveys, community assessments, third-party monitoring, and evaluations to generate evidence that informs effective programming and policy.

Our Methodology

How We Create Sustainable Change

Community Ownership

Programs are designed with community participation and local leadership to ensure relevance and sustainability.

Inclusion

Women, children, persons with disabilities, youth, and the elderly remain at the center of all our interventions.

Sustainability

Building local capacity to ensure long-term benefits continue beyond project implementation cycles.

Accountability

Maintaining transparency, independent audits, and responsible stewardship of all resources entrusted to us.

Professional Standards

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEL)

At NAVCA, we are committed to measuring what matters. Our MEL framework ensures we track progress, learn from experience, and continuously improve our programs.

By collecting community feedback, conducting assessments, and using evidence to guide decision-making, we ensure every intervention delivers meaningful, measurable results for the communities we serve.

Our MEL Approach

  • Measurable Indicators
