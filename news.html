Mental Health Disability Inclusion
March 16, 2026

NAVCA Raises Awareness on Mental Health and Disability Inclusion

NAVCA recently highlighted the importance of mental health and disability inclusion through community engagement activities aimed at promoting understanding, dignity, and equal opportunities for all.

The initiative encouraged stakeholders, community leaders, and families to recognize the challenges faced by persons with disabilities and advocate for greater access to support services. Mental health remains an often-overlooked issue in South Sudan, particularly among vulnerable populations affected by conflict, displacement, and poverty. NAVCA continues to champion awareness and inclusion as part of its commitment to human rights and social justice.

Gender Equality
March 10, 2026

Strengthening Community Action Against Gender-Based Violence

NAVCA continues to work alongside communities to address gender-based violence through awareness campaigns, advocacy initiatives, and community engagement activities. The organization promotes prevention, protection, and support mechanisms that help create safer environments for women and girls.

By engaging local leaders, youth groups, and community members, NAVCA aims to strengthen collective action and encourage positive social change.

Advocacy
March 5, 2026

Building Inclusive Communities Through Advocacy and Participation

Inclusive communities are stronger communities. Through advocacy and community participation initiatives, NAVCA continues to promote equal opportunities for women, youth, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups.

The organization believes lasting change is achieved when every individual has the opportunity to contribute to community development and decision-making processes.

Youth Empowerment
February 22, 2026

Youth Empowerment Through Skills Development

Young people play a critical role in shaping the future of South Sudan. NAVCA remains committed to supporting youth through skills development, entrepreneurship training, and leadership opportunities that help them become active contributors to their communities.

By investing in youth potential, NAVCA seeks to promote resilience, self-reliance, and sustainable development.

Peacebuilding
February 15, 2026

Community Dialogue Promotes Peace and Social Cohesion

Peaceful coexistence is essential for sustainable development. NAVCA continues to support community dialogue initiatives that encourage understanding, cooperation, and reconciliation among diverse groups.

These conversations help strengthen trust, reduce tensions, and foster stronger relationships between communities.

Disability Inclusion
February 1, 2026

Advancing the Rights of Persons with Disabilities

NAVCA remains dedicated to promoting the rights and inclusion of persons with disabilities across South Sudan. Through advocacy, awareness campaigns, and community engagement, the organization works to challenge stigma and encourage equal participation in education, livelihoods, and community life.

Creating an inclusive society requires collective effort, and NAVCA continues to advocate for a future where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
